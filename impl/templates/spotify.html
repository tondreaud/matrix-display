Spotify logins expire about every 6 months. Reconnecting takes under a minute.

Reconnect Spotify
  1. Tap Reconnect Spotify and log in / approve in Spotify.
  2. The browser will then try to open 127.0.0.1 and show a “cannot connect” page. That is expected.
  3. Copy the whole address from the address bar.
  4. Paste it below and tap Connect.
